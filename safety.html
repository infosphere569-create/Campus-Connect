Safety & Community Guidelines

Keeping campus digital spaces constructive, inclusive, and protected for everyone.

Live Moderation Safeguards
Instant Reporting Click the report flag on any post or message to send it directly to campus moderators.
Automated Filters Proactive toxicity scanning blocks profanity and spam before it reaches public feeds.
Emergency Helpline Direct access to campus security and student anti-ragging support helplines.

1. Reporting Inappropriate Content

If you encounter harmful content, harassment, or spam on Campus Connect One:

  1. Click or tap the Report action button on the post or message.
  2. Select the reason (Harassment, Misinformation, Spam, Inappropriate Media).
  3. Our automated queue flags the item for review by designated campus student moderators and platform administrators.

2. Campus Anti-Ragging & Anti-Harassment Policy

Campus Connect strictly enforces zero-tolerance policies against ragging, discrimination based on gender, branch, or background, and hate speech.

3. Emergency & Mental Health Resources

Need immediate help?

Contact Campus Security at 1800-CAMPUS-SAFE or student counselling services in the Student Activity Centre.
