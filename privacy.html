PRIVACY & TRUST MODEL

Student Privacy Policy

Campus Connect One is engineered from the ground up to respect student identity, prevent unwanted tracking, and safeguard anonymous voices.

Privacy Standard 2026–2027
100% Pseudonymous Anonymous issue reporting completely separates public posts from student profile IDs.
Zero Telemetry No third-party ad trackers, cross-site cookies, or behavioral profiling.
Student Data Ownership You can request export or permanent deletion of your profile data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver a verified campus community experience:

  • Account Identity: Your name, student email address, academic branch, and graduation year provided during signup.
  • Profile Customization: Optional bio, avatar image, and club memberships you choose to publish.
  • Content Contributions: Posts, group messages, event RSVPs, and issue votes submitted across the platform.

2. Anonymous Issues Identity Boundary

When you submit a Campus Issue anonymously:

Pseudonymity Guarantee:

The public issue document contains only the placeholder "Anonymous Student". Public users and non-staff members cannot view your real profile ID on anonymous complaints.

3. Data Protection & Security

All student communications use encrypted HTTPS in transit and granular Firestore security rules on database documents. We enforce strict role-based access control (RBAC) so that private records remain restricted.

4. Data Control & Deletion

You own your content. You can edit or delete your posts, leave groups, or request complete account erasure by contacting campus administration or via the Account Preferences menu.
