FAQ

Good to know

Do I need a new account?

No. Your Campus Connect One login works here. New to the platform? Create an account on the login page.

Can I be a participant and an organiser?

Yes, you choose your role when you log in. To switch roles, log out and log in again with the other role.

Can I share a hackathon link on Instagram or WhatsApp?

Yes. Every hackathon has its own public page URL showing complete details, schedule and prizes. Anyone can preview it directly and sign in when ready to register.

Which updates will I get?

You receive instant in-app notifications and email updates when your team is shortlisted, selected, reviewed, or when organisers post announcements.

How does "nearest" work?

With your permission we use your device location. You can also choose your city. Your location is never shown to others.
