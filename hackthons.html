FAQ

Good to know

Do I need a new account?

No. Your Campus Connect One login works here. New to the platform? Create an account on the login page.

Can I be a participant and an organiser?

Yes, but you choose one side each time you log in. To switch, log out and log in again with the other role.

Why does a hackathon need approval?

To keep the platform trustworthy. Admins check dates, details and contact info before a hackathon becomes visible to students.

Which emails will I get?

A confirmation when you register and updates when the organiser changes your status or moves you to the next round. They come from team@campusconnectone.online and are signed by the organiser.

How does "nearest" work?

With your permission we use your device location. You can also choose your city. Your location is never shown to others.
