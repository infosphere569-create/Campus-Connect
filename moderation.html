Campus Moderation Framework

A balanced multi-tiered moderation approach combining automated sentiment filters, peer reporting, and student council oversight.

Active Moderation Protocol
Automated Scanning Blocks profanity, hate speech keywords, and duplicate spam before publication.
Peer Review Queue Student council members and elected club leaders review reported posts in real time.
Immutable Audit Log Every warning, post removal, or suspension is recorded in the admin audit log for transparency.

How Moderation Works

1. Reporting an Item

Any student can report content using the flag button. Items receiving multiple reports are automatically placed in the moderation queue.

2. Moderator Review & Action

Moderators evaluate the context against our Safety Guidelines. Actions include approving, issuing a user warning, hiding the post, or temporary suspension.

3. Appeals Process

If your post was removed or flagged by mistake, you can submit an appeal through the Account Settings or Report Status tab.
